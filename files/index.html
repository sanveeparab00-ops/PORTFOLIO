        Frequently Asked Questions
        
Answers for leadership & engineering teams

      

      

        

          How quickly can our company be onboarded to DPex?
          
Most organizations are fully operational on DPex within 3 to 7 business days. We map your current Excel files, dump formats, and hospital portals directly into DPex without requiring any downtime or workflow stoppage.

        

        

          Can DPex integrate with our existing TPA or Hospital Portals?
          
Yes. DPex supports flexible REST APIs, custom webhook ingestion, and automated portal scrapers/RPA to sync admission pre-authorizations, claims, and invoices automatically.

        

        

          How secure is our confidential employee and healthcare data?
          
Security is baked into our architecture. All sensitive data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. We enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC), multi-tenant isolation, and complete audit logging.

        

        

          Can we deploy DPex on our own private cloud or on-premise servers?
          
Yes. While DPex is available as a turnkey managed cloud solution, enterprise clients can deploy DPex in their private AWS, Azure, GCP, or on-premise Docker environments with custom compliance configurations.
